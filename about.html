about will&way

Whether it's your morning coffee or a shared meal, I hope my ceramics bring small moments of connection and calm into your day.

my story

I spent years working as a software engineer, building systems through logic and precision. I loved the problem-solving, but something was missing—the physical, the tactile, the permanent.

In the evenings, I found myself in pottery classes, learning to coil, shape, and build with clay. For two years, I split my time: engineering by day, pottery by night.

Then I made the call. I set up a home studio, left tech, and committed to clay full-time. It turns out the skills translate—an engineer's mindset for testing, iterating, and problem-solving, but applied to something you can hold in your hands.

Now I'm building a practice rooted in experimentation and the quiet satisfaction of making tangible objects that people live with every day.

my process

I work exclusively with hand-building techniques—no wheel involved. Slabs, coils, pinching, scoring, joining. Each piece is constructed gradually, allowing the clay to firm up between stages. It's methodical work that requires patience.

My aesthetic leans on the geometric and architectural. I love the interplay of planes and angles, the way a straight edge meets a curve, how shadows fall across textured surfaces. Glazes are carefully chosen for their muted, variegated qualities—colours that shift and pool, creating depth without shouting for attention.

The work isn't perfect, and it's not meant to be. These are handmade objects. They carry the fingerprints of their making, and that's exactly what makes them worth living with.
