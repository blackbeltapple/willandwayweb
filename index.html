about will&way

I'm Amanda, the maker behind will&way ceramics.

After years as a software engineer, I made a decision: to leave a long-standing career and build something with my hands instead of code.

will&way represents that determination—the will to create, and the way I'm making it happen. This isn't a hobby that got out of hand. It's a deliberate choice to work with clay, to make objects that exist in the physical world, and to build a practice around slow, considered making.

why handmade matters

In a world of mass production, handmade ceramics invite you to slow down. To notice the weight of a mug, the curve of a vase, the quiet details that only exist because they were shaped by hand.

My pieces are made to be lived with – used, held, and cherished.
